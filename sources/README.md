# How the packs are made

Nothing here is needed to use the packs. This folder holds what they are built from and the scripts that build them.

- `build.py`: the pipeline, one subcommand per step (see its docstring for the commands): `export` the question sets from a checkout
  of thinkingtext's tt-news-index, which is not public (its cell registry and index specifications);
  `score` them on a spinf-serve over the calibration sample; `calibrate` into a pack (the mean and standard deviation of each read,
  no thresholds, no decision); `samples` to pick each pack's sample articles; `fixture` to score the samples; `manifest`, `readme`
  and `check`, which recomputes every fixture and is what CI runs.
- `wikinews_sample.py`: fetches the calibration sample from the Wikinews API.
- `drafts/<name>.draft.json`: the exported question sets before calibration, without the fitted constants. Regenerated by `export`;
  kept so a change in the index's registry shows as a diff.
- `calibration/wikinews_business.json.gz`: the calibration sample, 2,764 Wikinews business, economy, energy and company articles with
  each article's title, URL and revision (CC BY 2.5). `calibration/synthetic_samples.json`: illustrative articles written by
  thinkingtext for companies Wikinews rarely covers, used as shipped samples only when the Wikinews sample has fewer than three;
  every figure in them is invented. `calibration/answers/` (not committed): the raw scores the packs were standardised on.

A new index specification is a re-export and a new pack version; a published version never changes.
